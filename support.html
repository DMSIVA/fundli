Common Questions

Where is Fundli data stored?

The current version stores Fundli records locally on the device. Fundli does not currently provide cloud synchronization or shared online accounts.

How can I delete my Fundli data?

Open Fundli and select: Settings → Local Data Management → Delete All Local Data.

Does deleting Fundli delete its local records?

Deleting the app from the iPhone removes the app's locally stored data. Backups managed by Apple may follow the user's iCloud or device-backup settings.

Does Fundli connect to bank accounts?

No. The current version does not connect to banks, import bank credentials, or access payment-card accounts.
