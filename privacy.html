Fundli Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026
Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fundli handles information in the current iOS version of the app.

Summary

Fundli is designed as a local-first household budgeting application. The current version does not transmit the user's financial records to the developer or to a Fundli server.

Information Stored on the Device

Fundli may store information entered by the user, including:

  • Transaction titles, amounts, dates, and types
  • Income and expense categories
  • Monthly budget amounts and allocations
  • Family-member names and email addresses
  • Transaction and family-member assignments
  • Language, currency, and regional preferences
  • Onboarding and app-setting preferences

This information is stored locally using storage provided by iOS.

Information Fundli Does Not Currently Collect

The current version does not collect or transmit:

  • Bank usernames or passwords
  • Payment-card numbers
  • Government identification numbers
  • Precise location
  • Contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Analytics or cross-app tracking information

Bank Connections and Payments

Fundli does not currently connect to financial institutions, initiate payments, or process payment-card transactions.

Sharing and Third Parties

The current version does not sell personal information and does not share locally stored financial records with advertisers or data brokers.

Cloud Services

Fundli does not currently provide a Fundli cloud account or server-based synchronization. Apple's device backup services may handle app data according to the user's Apple and iCloud settings. Those services are governed by Apple's terms and privacy policies.

Data Retention and Deletion

Fundli records remain on the device until the user edits or deletes the records, uses Fundli's local data deletion feature, or deletes the application.

To delete records within the app, select: Settings → Local Data Management → Delete All Local Data.

Children's Privacy

Fundli is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Fundli relies on security protections provided by iOS and the user's device. No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should protect their devices with a passcode and keep iOS updated.

International Users

Fundli supports multiple regional formats and currencies. The current version stores records locally rather than transferring them to a Fundli server in another country.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when Fundli introduces features such as accounts, analytics, cloud synchronization, household sharing, or bank connectivity. The updated effective date will be published on this page.
